Q.2.  Write short answers of the following upto 25 lines maximum.  (10*6)

  1.  Compare and contrast tolerance and tachyphylaxis with examples in
      tabulated form.
  2.  Define plasma half life and how will you calculate it?
  3.  if A 52 year old farmer was spraying some pesticide in his fields where
      he lost consciousness. He was bought to emergency and was admitted with
      organophosphate poisoning.
        a. What is its symptomatic treatment?
        b. What are the specific antidotes available, give their mechanism of
           action
  4.  A 65 year old male complains of chest pain which is diffuse, sometimes
      radiates to the jaw. This pain is severe when he walks upstairs and is
      relieved on rest. Physician prescribes him nitrates to relieve his pain.
      Write in tabulated form the effect of nitrates alone and in presence of
      beta blockers. (3+3)
  5.  An epileptic lady had an attack of seizures was admitted in emergency.
      She was given injection of Diazepam to control her seizures. What would
      be possible mechanism of action and adverse effects of Diazepam?
  6.  Write down the mechanism of action and adverse effects of Omeprazole.
  7.  A 16-year-old boy (55 kg) presented to the emergency department of DHQ
      hospital with history of shortness of breath which is not controlled by
      the inhalers. How will you control acute attack of asthma in this
      patient?
  8.  Classify oral anti-diabetic drugs with examples.
  9.  Give mechanism of action and adverse effects of fluoroquinolones.
  10. What is the mechanism of action, clinical uses and side effects of
      Phenytoin?
